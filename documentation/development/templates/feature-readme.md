## What I want
What should the tool be able to do when this is done? Describe it the way you'd say it out loud.

## Why
What problem does it solve, or what will it make easier?

## Must have
- The things it is useless without.

## Nice to have

## Not now
- Things that are out of scope for this piece of work.

## Done when

job's status becomes `approved`."
